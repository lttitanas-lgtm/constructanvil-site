Privacy Policy

ConstructAnvil provides digital website services and B2B construction sourcing and commercial introduction services in the UK. Privacy enquiries can be sent to hello@constructanvil.co.uk.

We may collect business contact details, company information, website briefs, project requirements, trade and service information, geographic coverage, availability, accreditation or competency evidence supplied to us, correspondence, project approvals and commercial information needed to assess an introduction or deliver a website project.

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare website concepts and builds, manage client projects, assess construction requirements, identify relevant delivery partners, make requested B2B introductions, maintain business records, prevent misuse and comply with legal obligations.

4. Lawful basis

Depending on the circumstances, processing may rely on legitimate interests, steps taken at your request before entering a contract, performance of a contract, legal obligations or consent where specifically required.

We only share information where reasonably required for requested service delivery, a relevant commercial introduction, professional advice, legal compliance or trusted service providers acting on our behalf. We do not sell personal data as a consumer data product.

6. Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably required for the purpose collected, commercial record keeping, dispute handling and legal or regulatory obligations.

We use proportionate technical and organisational measures to protect business and personal information. No internet system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Cookies and analytics

The public website is designed to operate without non-essential advertising cookies. If analytics, tracking or advertising technology is introduced, this policy and any required cookie controls will be updated before activation.

9. Your rights

UK data protection law may give you rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability, subject to applicable exemptions.
