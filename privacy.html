Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Current scope: This policy covers ConstructAnvil construction sourcing/introduction services and ConstructAnvil Digital website services. Business identity and ICO details will be added where legally required and once formally confirmed.

1. Who we are

ConstructAnvil is a UK B2B construction sourcing and commercial introduction service and a digital website service for trades, contractors and other businesses.

2. Information we may collect

We may collect business contact details, company information, project requirements, trade and service information, geographic coverage, availability, accreditation or competency evidence supplied to us, correspondence, and commercial information needed to assess a possible introduction or deliver a website project, including website briefs, supplied content and project approvals.

3. Why we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, assess construction requirements, identify relevant delivery partners, make requested B2B introductions, prepare and deliver website services, manage commercial relationships, maintain records, prevent misuse and comply with legal obligations.

4. Lawful basis

Depending on the circumstances, processing may rely on legitimate interests, steps taken at your request before entering a contract, performance of a contract, legal obligations or consent where specifically required.

5. Sharing

We only share information where reasonably required for a requested or relevant commercial introduction, service delivery, professional advice, legal compliance or trusted service providers acting on our behalf. We do not sell personal data as a consumer data product.

6. Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably required for the purpose collected, commercial record keeping, dispute handling and legal or regulatory obligations.

7. Security

We use proportionate technical and organisational measures to protect business and personal information. No internet system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Cookies and analytics

The website is designed to operate without non-essential analytics or advertising cookies. If analytics, tracking or advertising technology is introduced, this policy and cookie controls must be updated before activation.

9. Your rights

UK data protection law may give you rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability, subject to applicable exemptions.

10. Contact

Privacy enquiries: hello@constructanvil.co.uk. This is the operational privacy contact address.

11. Regulator

You may have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. ConstructAnvil's final ICO registration details will be added here if registration is required.

This website policy is operational drafting and is not a substitute for tailored legal advice where required.
