Common questions

Before you send the brief.

Is the free concept really no obligation?

Yes. It is an initial homepage direction. You only move into the paid build after you approve the direction and scope in writing.

Do you use templates?

We may use proven layout patterns, but the structure, copy direction, service hierarchy and enquiry path are tailored to the business rather than sold as a generic screenshot.

Can you work with an existing website?

Yes. Redesigns, landing pages and focused rebuilds can be scoped around the existing site rather than starting from zero.

Who owns the finished site?

Ownership or hand-off terms are confirmed in the written project scope. Paid third-party licences and services remain subject to their own terms.
