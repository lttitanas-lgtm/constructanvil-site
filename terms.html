Website & Service Terms

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Scope: These terms cover ConstructAnvil's B2B construction sourcing/introduction services and ConstructAnvil Digital website services. Project-specific written terms or proposals take priority where they expressly differ from these website terms.

1. Role of ConstructAnvil

ConstructAnvil provides business-to-business sourcing, market intelligence, matching, commercial introduction and digital website services. Unless expressly agreed in a separate written contract, ConstructAnvil is not the contractor performing construction works, does not supervise site works and does not act as principal designer, principal contractor, employer's agent or contract administrator.

2. Buyer responsibility

Construction buyers remain responsible for their own due diligence, procurement decisions, competency checks, references, insurance checks, accreditations, financial checks, contract terms, health and safety obligations and project-specific compliance.

3. Contractor and supplier responsibility

Contractors and suppliers are responsible for the accuracy of information they provide, their competence, pricing, availability, insurance, certifications, statutory compliance and delivery of any work they accept.

4. Introductions

An introduction does not constitute a recommendation, warranty or guarantee of appointment, performance, payment, project award or commercial outcome. Parties decide independently whether to proceed.

5. Verification language

ConstructAnvil will only describe credentials, accreditations or evidence as verified where relevant evidence has actually been checked. Verification is point-in-time and does not replace the buyer's own checks.

6. Construction commercial fees

Any introduction fee, sourcing fee, retained search fee, success fee, commission or margin must be agreed in writing with the relevant contracting party before it becomes payable. Website content alone does not create a construction fee obligation.

7. ConstructAnvil Digital — free concept

A free homepage concept or page direction is an initial design direction only. It is provided without obligation to purchase a full website and may use draft copy, placeholder imagery or provisional structure until the client supplies final content and approves the full scope.

8. ConstructAnvil Digital — packages, promotions and payment

Founding-client launch pricing is £249 for Trade Starter (one-page website), £495 for Business (up to five pages) and £795 for Growth (up to ten pages). These launch prices are available to the first three verified paid website clients. A founding-client place is only treated as used after the agreed deposit has been verified as received. Once all three places are used, ConstructAnvil may review pricing before issuing new quotes while honouring any written quote already accepted within its validity period.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, no build deposit is due before written concept or scope approval. After approval, 50% of the agreed project price is due before the full build begins and the remaining 50% is due before public launch or hand-off. ConstructAnvil issues a private numbered invoice payable by the payment method stated on that invoice. Bank details, where used, are supplied privately and are not published on the public website or included in cold outreach. Payment is treated as received only after confirmed receipt.

Optional hosting and care is £29 per month where selected. Domain costs, paid third-party licences and specialist services are separate unless expressly included in writing.

9. Digital scope changes

Complex e-commerce, custom integrations, specialist booking systems, paid plugins, extensive copywriting, photography, branding, migration, large product catalogues or material changes after scope approval may require a separate written quote. No additional charge becomes payable unless agreed before that additional work is carried out.

10. Client content and approvals

Digital clients are responsible for supplying accurate business information, logos, images, legal notices and other content they want included, and for confirming they have the right to use supplied materials. The client is responsible for final factual approval before launch.

11. Demo concepts and portfolio

Any page clearly labelled as a demo or concept is illustrative work created by ConstructAnvil Digital and is not represented as a completed client project. Verified client work will only be described as such where the relationship and project are genuine.

12. Public opportunities

Public tenders, frameworks, awards and notices may be used as market intelligence or demand signals. ConstructAnvil does not claim public opportunities are exclusive unless there is separate evidence supporting exclusivity.

13. Information accuracy

We aim to use reliable information but project circumstances, availability, public data and third-party platform details can change quickly. Users should independently confirm material information before relying on it commercially.

14. Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, ConstructAnvil is not responsible for losses arising from decisions made solely from website information or from the acts, omissions, defaults or performance of independent buyers, contractors, suppliers or third-party service providers. Nothing excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.

15. Intellectual property

The ConstructAnvil name, website copy, design system and original materials are proprietary to the ConstructAnvil business, subject to third-party rights. For paid digital projects, ownership or licence terms for the final website are confirmed in the project proposal or hand-off terms.

General and digital enquiries: hello@constructanvil.co.uk. Construction commercial enquiries: deals@constructanvil.co.uk.

For high-value, regulated or unusual transactions, tailored written terms may be required before work proceeds.
